# Project Charter v2

Date: 2025-11-18
Owner: Sponsor
Status: Approved

## Purpose

Operate GPL as the governed internal distribution of Google Guava, with quarterly releases and enterprise governance.

## Budget and team

Budget 1.6 million currency units, team of 12 engineers, first release of Guava 33.x scheduled for 2026-02-27.

## Objectives

Reach 90 percent adoption across Java services by 2026-12-31, remove all Hashing.md5 use, and publish both flavors every release.

## Out of scope

Replacing the internal message broker, forking Guava sources and supporting Kotlin-only libraries.

## Open items

Open: adoption target for the Mobile Platform team is to decide.

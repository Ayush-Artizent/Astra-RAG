# Consumer Service Level Agreement

Date: 2026-01-30
Owner: Release Manager

## Availability of artifacts

The internal Maven repository hosting GPL targets 99.9 percent monthly availability.

## Support

Platform team answers consumer tickets within 2 business days and publishes a fix or workaround for critical defects within 5 business days.

## Deprecation notice

Consumers receive at least 12 months and two minor releases of notice before API removal, matching the deprecation policy.

# Stakeholder Register

Date: 2026-01-10
Owner: Program Manager

## Governance

Sponsor: Head of Platform Engineering, approves budget and charter changes. Governance Board chair: Program Manager. Compliance Lead: reviews license and SBOM obligations. Vendor Manager: manages the Northwind support agreement.

## Engineering roles

Platform Architect owns architecture and ADRs. Core Collections Lead owns collect, cache, base and util.concurrent. Security Architect owns hash, io and net reviews. QA Lead owns test standards. Release Manager owns the release train. Build Engineering Lead owns dependency management.

## Consumers

Order Services, Payments, Data Platform, Mobile Platform and Gateway teams consume GPL. Data Platform Lead is the largest consumer of BloomFilter and hashing. Mobile Platform Lead is the only owner of android flavor consumers.

## Influence and interest

High influence and high interest: Sponsor, Platform Architect, Security Architect. High interest and lower influence: consuming team leads. Open: add the Developer Experience team to the register is to decide.

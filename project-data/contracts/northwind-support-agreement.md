# Northwind Open Source Support Agreement

Date: 2025-07-01
Owner: Vendor Manager
Status: Active until 2027-06-30

## Parties and term

Between Acme Platform Engineering and Northwind Support Ltd (fictional vendor). Term 2025-07-01 to 2027-06-30 with one renewal option of 12 months.

## Response times

Critical Guava issues receive a response within 3 business days, high within 5 business days, medium within 10 business days. Note: internal rules state critical patches within 5 business days; the numbers differ.

## Scope

Covers Guava versions within 24 months of release and both jre and android flavors. Includes up to 40 support hours per quarter.

## Fees

Annual fee is 85,000 currency units, invoiced quarterly.

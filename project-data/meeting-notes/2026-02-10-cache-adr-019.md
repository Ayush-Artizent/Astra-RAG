# Meeting Notes: Cache default sizing review

Date: 2026-02-10
Owner: Platform Architect
Attendees: Platform Architect; Core Collections Lead; QA Lead; Product Owner

## Benchmark results

QA Lead presented JMH results. At 25,000 entries the p99 load latency was 4.1 ms and hit ratio 91 percent. At 10,000 entries hit ratio was 83 percent.

## Decision

ADR-019 accepted: default maximumSize 25,000 and concurrencyLevel remains 4 until CR-2026-017 is approved. ADR-014 is superseded.

## Open items

Open: expose a weigher-based sizing option to teams caching values over 64 KB.

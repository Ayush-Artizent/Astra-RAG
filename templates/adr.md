# Architecture Decision Record

Template for an architecture decision record.

## Title and Status

Give an id `ADR-nnn`, a status of Proposed, Accepted, or Superseded, and a `Date:` line.

## Context

Explain the forces and constraints.

## Decision

State the decision in one or two sentences.

## Consequences

List positive and negative consequences.

## Alternatives Considered

List rejected options with the reason.

# Business Requirements Document

Template for a business requirements document about the Click CLI Toolkit.

## Overview

State the product area, the sponsor role, and the date. Include `Date:` and `Owner:` lines.

## Business Objectives

List measurable objectives, such as reducing CLI boilerplate or raising shell completion coverage.

## Scope

Describe in-scope and out-of-scope capabilities.

## Requirements

Table with columns ID (REQ-nnn), Description, Priority, Source.

## Constraints and Assumptions

Include supported Python versions and dependency policy.

## Open Items

List each as `Open: ...` with an owner.
